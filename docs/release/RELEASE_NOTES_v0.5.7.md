# Research Collaboration Portal v0.5.7

## Updated

- Updates current Governed Research Workflow links and installation prompts to
  the published `v1.21.1` release.
- Adds aligned Chinese, English, and Japanese explanations of the new Study-root
  convention: `Research<number>-<lowercase-hyphenated-slug>`.
- Clarifies that a Study root, `study_id`, `study_title`, and Framework
  `research_program_id` are distinct, and that historical Study roots are not
  scanned, renamed, moved, or migrated.
- Includes the already merged Clinical Deidentifier `v0.2.0` component card and
  its Chinese, English, and Japanese method pages since the prior portal tag.

## Boundaries

- This release changes public portal documentation only.
- It does not create, inspect, rename, move, or migrate a real Study; it does
  not select a Study number, access data, publish research materials, or grant
  research, governance, or release authority.

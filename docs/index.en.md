<section class="portal-section role-explainer"><div class="section-header"><div><p class="section-context">BASIC CONCEPTS</p><h2>What are Frameworks, Systems, and Skills?</h2></div></div><div class="role-terminal"><article class="role-terminal-entry"><h3>Framework</h3><p><span aria-hidden="true">|-</span> Provides the workspace skeleton and the rules for where categories of material belong.</p><p><span aria-hidden="true">|-</span> Makes the workspace, shared services, projects, and public derivatives easier to recognize and maintain.</p><p><span aria-hidden="true">|_</span> Example: it gives Systems, Skills, Methods, Instances, and Knowledge separate homes.</p></article><article class="role-terminal-entry"><h3>System</h3><p><span aria-hidden="true">|-</span> Organizes a bounded kind of work into a route, records, and review points.</p><p><span aria-hidden="true">|-</span> Keeps its materials and decisions traceable as the work develops.</p><p><span aria-hidden="true">|_</span> Example: a Research System organizes a Study from request to archive.</p></article><article class="role-terminal-entry"><h3>Skills</h3><p><span aria-hidden="true">|-</span> Provide focused help for a named task, such as reading a paper or preparing a bounded ethics package.</p><p><span aria-hidden="true">|-</span> Enter only when the user explicitly asks for the task and its conditions are met.</p><p><span aria-hidden="true">|_</span> Examples: <code>research-paper-reading</code>, <code>research-ethics</code>, and Governed Engineering.</p></article></div></section>

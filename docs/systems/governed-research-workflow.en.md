<header class="component-header"><p class="component-kind">System</p><h1>Governed Research Workflow</h1><p>Organize a Study from its first idea and design through execution and results, then to a manuscript, revision, and archive.</p></header>

<section class="component-section component-summary system-summary"><p>Use this System when a research project needs durable records of its protocol, runs, results, manuscript, and revision. It keeps these materials inside the Study and preserves where important decisions came from.</p><div class="release-stamp"><span>Current public Release</span><strong><a href="https://github.com/chenhaoran2068/governed-research-workflow/releases/tag/v1.18.1">v1.18.1</a></strong></div></section>

<section class="component-section" aria-labelledby="study-status-title"><div class="section-header"><div><p class="section-context">NEW IN v1.18</p><h2 id="study-status-title">See where a Study stands</h2></div><p>Keep a short, reviewable current-state record in <code>00_state/study_status_snapshot.json</code>.</p></div><div class="boundary-grid"><div><h3>Record the operating status first</h3><p><code>queued</code> means waiting to start, <code>active</code> means work is progressing, <code>paused</code> means work is temporarily held and may resume, <code>stopped</code> means the current route has ended, and <code>archived</code> means the materials have been closed out and retained.</p></div><div><h3>State the current position when active</h3><p>An <code>active</code> Study also records its current 01–11 stage, current focus, and next action or human decision. A paused or stopped Study retains its reason and any condition for resuming.</p></div><div><h3>People and project evidence confirm the state</h3><p>The template and validator check structure and internal consistency only. They do not scan folders to infer status or prove that a stage is correct, a gate passed, or a decision was approved.</p></div></div></section>

<section class="component-section" aria-labelledby="paper-repository-title"><div class="section-header"><div><p class="section-context">REFINED IN v1.18.1</p><h2 id="paper-repository-title">Prepare a public materials repository for one paper</h2></div><p>By default, one paper or independently citable research output has one repository. Prepare it inside the Study first; a person decides whether it is released.</p></div><div class="boundary-grid"><div><h3>Choose a truthful release profile</h3><p>Depending on rights and data conditions, use a synthetic demonstration, redistributable data, separate data-access instructions, or a materials-only package. A public repository is not a copy of the whole Study and does not automatically include real data or real results.</p></div><div><h3>Keep the repository name short and stable</h3><p>Choose two or three stable elements for the research type from the subject or domain, core question, exposure or intervention, outcome, method, and output type. Keep the complete research question and any applicable PICOS or other design details in the README and Study summary.</p></div><div><h3>Build a clean candidate from an allowlist</h3><p>Record included and excluded material, code and data rights, execution steps, expected outputs, citation information, and versions. Build the candidate in a new clean directory; do not initialize the Study root itself as the Git repository.</p></div><div><h3>Move to Github/ only after review</h3><p>Release records and review evidence remain in the Study. After a person confirms the name, scope, and candidate, place only the verified clean candidate in <code>Github/&lt;repository-name&gt;/</code>. Do not copy the whole Study or synchronize the two locations.</p></div></div></section>

<section class="component-section"><div class="section-header"><div><p class="section-context">NEW IN v1.15</p><h2>Record the order of a joint review</h2></div><p>The default profile is for observational empirical original research.</p></div><div class="boundary-grid"><div><h3>A human chooses the profile first</h3><p>Confirm which review profile fits the work. The default profile is only for observational empirical original research. Trials, causal-effect studies, prediction models, systematic reviews, qualitative work, and methods papers need a separate specialist profile.</p></div><div><h3>Then record the review order</h3><p>Place design and governance facts, data definitions and execution, result authority, Results, Methods, Discussion, Introduction, abstract, and submission-facing material in a declared review plan. When a later change affects an earlier accepted package, record its reopening and review it again.</p></div><div><h3>It only manages plan records</h3><p>v1.15 provides blank templates, structural validation, and reopen metadata. It does not choose a profile, read a Study, data, code, results, or manuscript, or decide that a review has passed.</p></div></div></section>

<section class="component-section"><div class="section-header"><div><p class="section-context">NEW IN v1.16</p><h2>How manuscript requirements enter a Study</h2></div><p>Keep general writing rules, study-type reporting guidance, and target-journal requirements distinct, then use them in a declared order.</p></div><div class="boundary-grid"><div><h3>Record the current writing stack first</h3><p>During manuscript work, record the current requirements in <code>09_manuscript/drafting_requirement_stack.yaml</code>. Ordinary medical and health research may use <code>ama_11_default</code> as a general starting point. It uses publicly checkable AMA-derived principles only; it is not a claim that every rule in the full AMA Manual has been verified.</p></div><div><h3>Then add study-type and journal requirements</h3><p>Add the applicable reporting guideline for the study type. Once a target journal is chosen, add its current author, figure, reference, and declaration requirements. When a journal rule conflicts with the general writing profile, the current journal rule takes precedence.</p></div><div><h3>Return conflicts to a human decision</h3><p>A journal must not be used to silently remove reporting-guideline content. If the source, currency, or conflict is unclear, identify the gap and wait for human confirmation before changing the manuscript, figures, citations, or declarations.</p></div></div></section>

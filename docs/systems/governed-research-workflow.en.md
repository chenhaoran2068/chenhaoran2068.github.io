<header class="component-header"><p class="component-kind">System</p><h1>Governed Research Workflow</h1><p>Organize a Study from its first idea and design through execution and results, then to a manuscript, revision, and archive.</p></header>

<section class="component-section component-summary system-summary"><p>Use this System when a research project needs durable records of its protocol, runs, results, manuscript, and revision. It keeps these materials inside the Study and preserves where important decisions came from.</p><div class="release-stamp"><span>Current public Release</span><strong><a href="https://github.com/chenhaoran2068/governed-research-workflow/releases/tag/v1.14.0">v1.14.0</a></strong></div></section>

<section class="component-section"><div class="section-header"><div><p class="section-context">HUMAN–AI COLLABORATION ROUTE</p><h2>How a research Study progresses</h2></div><p>This route may pause, return, be redesigned, or stop. A positive or statistically significant early result is not an automatic reason to continue.</p></div><div class="system-route-map"><div class="system-route-board"><div class="system-route-trunk"><div class="system-route-primary"><span>01</span><strong>Research request</strong><small>For a new Study, open a new conversation and send: “I want to start a new research Study.”</small></div><div class="system-route-down" aria-hidden="true">↓</div><div class="system-route-primary"><span>02</span><strong>Confirm collaboration and workspace</strong><small>Choose human–AI collaboration or a bounded autonomous segment, then confirm the Study, workspace, and permitted materials.</small></div></div><div class="system-route-decision system-route-decision-two-way"><span><b>Human–AI collaboration</b> The default: you and AI confirm, advance, review, and close each step together.</span><span><b>Bounded autonomy</b> Only after the permitted input, actions, output, QA, stop conditions, and human gates are explicitly approved.</span></div></div><aside class="system-route-inspector"><p class="system-inspector-label">Skill boundary</p><h3>Skills enter by an explicit task</h3><p>A Skill is not automatically started by the System. At stage 05, <code>research-ethics</code> may be named when its conditions are met. During bounded implementation, the appropriate Governed Engineering Skill may be named.</p><p class="system-route-boundary">A reading Skill remains independently entered. If material is later handed to a Study, the handoff is metadata-only until a human makes a Study-specific application judgement.</p></aside></div></section>

<section class="component-section"><div class="section-header"><div><p class="section-context">03–05 · ESTABLISH THE STUDY APPROACH</p><h2>Question, design, and pre-study review</h2></div></div><div class="governance-grid"><article><h3>03 Confirm the research question</h3><p>If the question is clear, state the phenomenon, population or setting, relationship, and why it matters. If it is not clear, begin with the field, concern, available experience, and desired practical or academic contribution; external searching occurs only with permission.</p></article><article><h3>04 Confirm the research design</h3><p>Specify who enters the research, what is compared, what outcome is assessed, and the observation timeline. Also distinguish intervention assignment, selection design, time direction, data source, and research purpose. Write eligibility, sampling, or case/control rules as appropriate; define time zero, measurement windows, follow-up, and administrative censoring.</p></article><article><h3>05 Complete the protocol and pre-study review</h3><p>Turn the design into a full protocol, then complete the scientific review, ethics review, filing or registration, data access, and other materials required by the real institution, jurisdiction, and data holder. Name <code>research-ethics</code> only after the Study root, allowed material range, and deliverable are clear.</p></article></div></section>

<section class="component-section"><div class="section-header"><div><p class="section-context">06 · DECIDE WHETHER THE STUDY MERITS FULL INVESTMENT</p><h2>Run the key-part check</h2></div></div><div class="boundary-grid"><div><h3>Use the most decisive small part</h3><p>Within the permitted scope, test whether the data, definitions, time logic, and core result can support further work.</p></div><div><h3>Decide with the human owner</h3><p>Continue, continue with conditions, redesign, or stop. Conditions need a named resolution point; a preliminary check is not a final research conclusion.</p></div><div><h3>Lock before formal analysis</h3><p>If continuing, record the analysis state and freeze the executable version. A later correction, planned sensitivity analysis, exploration, or new validation needs its own reason and version.</p></div></div></section>

<section class="component-section"><div class="section-header"><div><p class="section-context">07–08 · COMPLETE THE RESEARCH MATERIALS</p><h2>Results first, then the rest of the manuscript</h2></div></div><div class="governance-grid"><article><h3>07 Complete Results</h3><p>First settle the structure for primary, secondary, sensitivity, and exploratory results. Check the whole Results section, sections, paragraphs, sentences and claims, figures/tables, and the underlying evidence in order. A figure, table, and sentence must agree before review.</p></article><article><h3>Then complete the remaining manuscript and submission materials</h3><p>Methods returns to the protocol and actual execution. Discussion and Conclusion interpret the settled results and limits. Then complete Introduction, Abstract or Summary, declarations, supplements, cover letter, and later response materials.</p></article><article><h3>08 Review all materials together</h3><p>Check the current protocol, governance evidence, runs, results, manuscript, figures, claims, declarations, and submission package together. Route a problem back to the affected stage instead of carrying an old review conclusion forward.</p></article></div></section>

<section class="component-section"><div class="section-header"><div><p class="section-context">09–11 · SUBMISSION, REVISION, AND ARCHIVE</p><h2>Close the research responsibly</h2></div></div><div class="governance-grid"><article><h3>09 Prepare the submission package</h3><p>Choose the journal and article type, then check the journal's current requirements and let the human owner decide whether to submit.</p></article><article><h3>10 Respond to editorial decisions and reviews</h3><p>Classify each comment, decide what to change or not change and why, make the corresponding material change, and re-review affected methods, results, claims, or declarations.</p></article><article><h3>11 Archive and reflect</h3><p>Handle proofs, rights, open access, final material, and any post-publication correction. Project reflection remains local to the Study until separately reviewed for broader reuse.</p></article></div></section>

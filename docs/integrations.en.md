<header class="portal-header"><p class="portal-context">EXTERNAL INTEGRATIONS</p><h1>Optional tools with explicit boundaries</h1><p>Frameworks, Systems, and Skills can work alongside external tools. An external tool does not acquire a Study, data, or decision-making role simply because it is installed.</p></header>

<section class="portal-section"><div class="section-header"><div><p class="section-context">Verified optional integration</p><h2>Zotero for papers and reading</h2></div><p>Zotero owns bibliographic items and, where permitted, attached PDFs.</p></div><div class="integration-flow"><article class="integration-flow-node"><span>01</span><div><h3>One named paper</h3><p>State the source, the reason to read it, and whether download or retention is allowed.</p></div></article><div class="integration-flow-arrow" aria-hidden="true"><span>↓</span><small>explicit source-specific permission</small></div><article class="integration-flow-node"><span>02</span><div><h3>Zotero</h3><p>Manage the bibliographic item and its permitted PDF; retain the manager's item identity.</p></div></article><div class="integration-flow-arrow" aria-hidden="true"><span>↓</span></div><article class="integration-flow-node"><span>03</span><div><h3><code>Knowledge/</code></h3><p>Keep a reading record and source-backed knowledge candidates, not a second unmanaged PDF collection.</p></div></article><div class="integration-flow-arrow integration-flow-optional" aria-hidden="true"><span>↓</span><small>only when a named Study needs it</small></div><article class="integration-flow-node"><span>04</span><div><h3><code>&lt;Study&gt;/04_knowledge/</code></h3><p>Record a Study-specific application judgement. Study experience stays in <code>05_memory/</code>.</p></div></article></div><div class="integration-notes"><p>No PDF, library item, tag, knowledge card, experience, or rule is created automatically merely because a reading conversation occurred.</p><p>Knowledge derived from a paper may propose an experience or rule candidate, but it is not transferred or promoted automatically.</p><p>For the reading route and its source boundary, see <a href="skills/research-paper-reading/">research-paper-reading</a>.</p></div></section>

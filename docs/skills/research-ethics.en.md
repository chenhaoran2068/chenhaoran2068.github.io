<header class="component-header"><p class="component-kind">Skill</p><h1>research-ethics</h1><p>Prepare a reviewable protocol skeleton and platform-ready draft materials for mainland-China observational medical research, without claiming that a Study has been approved or registered.</p></header>

<section class="component-section component-summary"><p>This Skill is a conditional part of research preparation. Use it only after the Study, the intended material range, and the requested deliverable have been made clear. It helps arrange and check materials; actual ethics, filing, registration, and data-access status come only from the relevant institution, data holder, or platform.</p><div class="release-stamp"><span>Current public Release</span><strong><a href="https://github.com/chenhaoran2068/research-ethics/releases/tag/v1.1.1">v1.1.1</a></strong></div></section>

<section class="component-section"><div class="section-header"><div><p class="section-context">When it can be used</p><h2>Enter deliberately at System stage 05</h2></div></div><div class="ethics-preparation-flow"><article><span>01</span><div><h3>Study is specified</h3><p>Provide the Study root, the allowed materials, and the intended output. A general idea is not yet an ethics package.</p></div></article><article><span>02</span><div><h3>Build a protocol skeleton</h3><p>Lay out the question, population, design, variables, timeline, analysis plan, and relevant governance issues.</p></div></article><article><span>03</span><div><h3>Review gaps with a human</h3><p>Identify unsupported fields, missing documents, and statements that need confirmation before they are put into a form.</p></div></article><article><span>04</span><div><h3>Prepare platform-ordered drafts</h3><p>After confirmation, create copyable drafts and attachment checklists in the order required by the named route.</p></div></article></div></section>

<section class="component-section"><div class="section-header"><div><p class="section-context">Scope</p><h2>What this version covers</h2></div></div><div class="boundary-grid"><div><h3>Supported</h3><p>Mainland-China researcher-initiated observational studies, including a coded protocol skeleton, coverage checks, material lists, and paired Chinese/English registry content where applicable.</p></div><div><h3>Needs a later specialist route</h3><p>Interventional studies, product registration, and routes outside this scope are not treated as supported simply because a form can be drafted.</p></div><div><h3>Never inferred</h3><p>Ethics approval, consent waiver, registry completion, access rights, actual submission, platform upload, and factual Study status.</p></div></div></section>

<section class="component-section"><div class="section-header"><div><p class="section-context">Where records belong</p><h2>Keep preparation distinct from evidence and decisions</h2></div></div><div class="ethics-storage-tree"><p><code>03_protocol/derived/ethics_preparation/&lt;package_id&gt;/</code><span># generated preparation drafts and checklists</span></p><p><code>02_registry/compliance/</code><span># source evidence, receipts, and the supported current state</span></p><p><code>00_state/lifecycle/</code><span># human decisions and references to the supporting material</span></p></div><p>Preparation drafts do not overwrite the current protocol, and a prepared package is not a submitted package.</p></section>
